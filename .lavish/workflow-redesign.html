Kewy CRM · Workflows · Redesign proposal

Make the workflow builder readable at a glance

Today you have to click every step to find out what a workflow does. This proposal turns the builder into a vertical flow: each step shows what it does, you can add a step anywhere, and the settings panel only appears once you've picked a step. It uses the existing step types, triggers and data model, so the backend doesn't change.

02Three directions

All three keep the current data model (entry + next / thenNext / elseNext). They differ in layout and in how much work they are.

A · Vertical flow + settings panel Recommended

Steps go top to bottom from the trigger, with a + between every pair. A condition splits into Yes/No lanes. Clicking a step opens its settings in a right-hand panel.

Fits
Your graph shape: mostly straight lines with occasional yes/no splits. Familiar from HubSpot, Klaviyo and Zapier.
Gains
Fixes all 7 problems. Readable without clicking. Reuses the existing config forms.
Cost
About 3–4 days. Rewrites WorkflowBuilder, StepCard and AddStepButton, and adds insert/delete-anywhere logic to the graph.

B · Free-form canvas (n8n-style)

Drag steps anywhere, draw connections, zoom and pan, with a minimap.

Fits
Complex graphs with merges, loops and many outputs, which the data model doesn't support today.
Gains
Looks powerful. Leaves room for future step types.
Cost
2–3 weeks and a new dependency such as React Flow. Saved layout positions would need a schema change. Harder for non-technical users and on small screens, and the canvas needs RTL work.

C · Restyle the current list

Keep today's layout, but apply the app's styles: styled cards with summaries, a proper step menu, and the settings panel shown below the selected card.

Fits
A quick fix while the bigger redesign waits.
Gains
About 1 day. Fixes problems 1, 3, 4 and 7.
Cost
Branches still read poorly and you still can't add in the middle (problems 2, 5 and 6 stay). You'd probably redo it later.

03Direction A, clickable

Click any step to open its settings. Click a + to open the step picker. Annotate anything you'd change.

Example: Welcome new leads. A new contact gets a welcome message if they're a lead, and a tag otherwise.

Details the mockup shows

  • Each step's colour and icon match its group: Messages (green), AI (violet), Logic (blue), CRM data (orange).
  • Each card shows a one-line summary built from its settings, e.g. "Lifecycle is Lead" or "Hi {contact.name}…".
  • An incomplete step gets an orange border and a reason. The header counts these and keeps Activate disabled until they're fixed.
  • The settings panel has a readable title, the same form fields as today, a WhatsApp message preview, and "Delete step" for any step. Deleting a middle step reconnects its neighbours.
  • The layout uses logical properties (inline-start), so Arabic mirrors the panel and lanes, as the Automations wizard already does.
