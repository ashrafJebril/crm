# Compliance / due-diligence email to Zernio

Send before routing any customer data through Zernio. Per our decision, adoption is gated on acceptable answers here.

---

**Subject:** Pre-adoption due diligence — data residency, PDPL/GDPR, and platform-app ownership

Hi Zernio team,

We're evaluating Zernio to power social + WhatsApp messaging for our CRM, whose customers are primarily in Saudi Arabia / the GCC. Before we route any customer message data through your platform, we need written answers on the following:

**Platform apps & connect flow (most important):**
1. For Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and Snapchat, do customer account connections run entirely through **Zernio-owned, already-reviewed platform apps** — such that our customers need **no Meta App Review and no Tester enrollment** on any app we own? Please confirm explicitly per platform.
2. For WhatsApp: can Zernio **provision a new business phone number** (including in Saudi Arabia / GCC) tied to the customer's WABA via Embedded Signup? What is the KYC process, typical activation time, and monthly number cost for a Saudi number?

**Data protection & residency:**
3. Where is customer message content (DMs, WhatsApp) and media stored and processed geographically? Is an EU or GCC region available? Is content stored at rest, or proxied only?
4. What are your retention periods for message content, media, and OAuth access tokens? Can we opt into minimal/zero retention (pass-through only)?
5. Saudi PDPL: will you sign a Data Processing Agreement (DPA)? How do you support data-subject rights, breach notification, and cross-border transfer safeguards?
6. Do you hold SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, or GDPR compliance? Can you share reports/attestations under NDA?
7. Please list your sub-processors that touch message content (we understand Metronome + Stripe handle billing).

**Continuity & commercial:**
8. Given the May 2026 pricing change, what advance notice do you give existing customers before price changes, and are prices grandfathered?
9. What is your uptime SLA, and what data-export path exists if we offboard?
10. Who operates Zernio (legal entity and jurisdiction), and what is the relationship to the former Getlate.dev product?

Thanks,
tkana / Aram team

---

*Answers land in project memory once received; do not migrate customer data until items 3–7 are satisfactory.*
