Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026 · Last updated 18 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kewy (“Kewy”, “we”, “us”), the operator of Kewy Marketing at marketing.kewy.ai and kewy.ai (the “Service”), collects, uses, shares and deletes personal data. Kewy Marketing is a customer-relationship and marketing platform that lets businesses manage WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger and Instagram conversations, publish social content, run campaigns and use AI assistants in one place.

In short: we use data only to run the Service for the business that connected it. We do not sell personal data, we do not use data received from Meta for advertising or profiling, and you can delete your connection data at any time (see Data deletion).

1. Who is responsible

Kewy acts in two roles:

  • As controller for the data of people who sign up for and use the Service (account holders and their team members), and for billing and usage data.
  • As processor (service provider) for the data a business puts into, or receives through, its workspace: its contacts, customers' messages, comments, posts and campaign data. That business (our “Customer”) decides why and how this data is processed. If you are a customer of a business that uses Kewy Marketing and want to access or delete your messages or contact details, please contact that business directly; we will help them respond.

2. What we collect

Account data

Name, email address, password (stored only as a salted hash), workspace name, role, language and preferences of the people who use the Service.

Workspace content

Data Customers add or receive in the Service: contacts (names, phone numbers, emails, tags, notes), conversations and messages, comments, media files, social posts and schedules, pipeline deals, campaigns, templates, workflows and AI agent settings and knowledge documents.

Connected-platform data

When a Customer connects a channel (WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram or another supported platform) we receive the data described in section 3 and in that platform's permission screen.

Billing data

Plan, invoices and payment status. Card details are entered with and held by our payment processor, Stripe; we do not store full card numbers.

Technical data

IP address, browser type, device information, timestamps and error logs, used to keep the Service secure and working. We use browser storage (cookies and local storage) only to keep you signed in and remember preferences such as language and theme. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

3. Data from Meta platforms (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp)

A Customer can connect their Facebook Page, Instagram professional account and WhatsApp Business Account to Kewy Marketing through Facebook Login for Business or WhatsApp Embedded Signup. Only the accounts and permissions the Customer approves in Meta's dialog are shared with us. Depending on those permissions we receive and store:

  • Connection data: access tokens (encrypted at rest), the app-scoped user ID of the person who connected, business, Page, Instagram account and WhatsApp Business Account IDs, phone number IDs, display phone numbers, verified business names and Page/account names and pictures.
  • Messaging data: messages sent to and from the Customer's Page, Instagram account or WhatsApp number, including the sender's name or profile name, profile ID or phone number, message content, attachments and delivery/read status.
  • Content and engagement data: posts the Customer publishes or schedules, comments on them and the commenter's public name and ID, and Page/account insights such as reach and engagement.
  • WhatsApp management data: message templates and their approval status, phone number quality and messaging limits.

We use this data only to provide the features the Customer uses: showing and replying to conversations in the shared inbox, publishing and scheduling posts, replying to comments, sending template messages the Customer starts, AI-drafted or automated replies the Customer turns on, and showing analytics. We:

  • do not sell, rent or license Meta platform data;
  • do not use it for advertising, ad targeting, profiling, or building user profiles across Customers;
  • do not share it with anyone except the service providers listed in section 6 who help us run the Service, or when required by law;
  • do not use it to train AI models;
  • comply with the Meta Platform Terms, the Developer Policies and the WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy.

4. How we use data

  • To provide, operate and support the Service and the features each Customer chooses.
  • To authenticate users and secure accounts.
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions.
  • To send service messages (for example security alerts, billing notices and product changes).
  • To monitor, debug and improve reliability and performance.
  • To comply with law and enforce our Terms of Service.

Our legal bases are performing our contract with the Customer, our legitimate interest in running a secure and reliable service, the Customer's instructions (for workspace content), consent where required, and legal obligations.

5. AI features

When a Customer turns on AI features (such as AI reply suggestions, the AI agent, or content generation), the relevant parts of a conversation, the Customer's knowledge documents and instructions are sent to an AI model provider (OpenAI or Anthropic) to generate a response. These providers process the data on our behalf under API terms that do not allow them to use it to train their models. Customers decide whether AI features are on and should review AI output before relying on it.

7. How long we keep data

  • Connection data (tokens and account IDs) is kept while the channel is connected and deleted when it is disconnected, when the app is removed from the person's Facebook settings, or on a deletion request.
  • Workspace content is kept for as long as the Customer's workspace is active, and deleted within 30 days after the Customer closes the workspace or asks us to delete it, except where we must keep it by law.
  • Account and billing data is kept while the account is active and afterwards for as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Logs are kept for up to 90 days.

8. Data deletion

You can have your data deleted in any of these ways:

  1. Disconnect in the app: in Kewy Marketing go to Settings → Integrations and choose Disconnect on the WhatsApp, Facebook or Instagram card. The access tokens and account IDs for that channel are deleted immediately.
  2. Remove the app in Facebook: go to Facebook Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites (or Business integrations), find Kewy Marketing and choose Remove. Meta notifies us, we delete the connection data linked to your account automatically, and you can check the status with the confirmation code you receive.
  3. Email us at info@kewy.ai with the subject “Data deletion request” and the email address or Facebook account involved. We will confirm your identity and complete the request within 30 days.

Deleting a Meta connection removes the access tokens and account identifiers we hold for it. Messages and contacts stored in a business's workspace are that business's records; to have those deleted, contact the business, or ask us and we will pass your request to them. Workspace owners can delete their entire workspace and account by emailing info@kewy.ai.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Platform access tokens and other secrets are encrypted at rest. Access to production systems is limited to staff who need it, and every workspace's data is isolated from other workspaces. No system is perfectly secure; if we become aware of a breach affecting your data we will notify affected Customers and authorities as the law requires.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, email info@kewy.ai. If your data is held in a business's workspace, we will refer your request to that business. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

11. International transfers

Our servers are in the European Union, and some service providers process data in other countries, including the United States. Where the law requires it, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

12. Children

The Service is for businesses and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the “Last updated” date above and, for significant changes, notify workspace owners by email or in the app before the change takes effect.
